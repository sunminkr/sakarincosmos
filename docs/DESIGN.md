## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, tactile digital archive for an independent music collective. Rooted in late-night basement rehearsals, photocopied self-published zines, physical cassette inlay cards, and quiet astronomical curiosity, the visual atmosphere balances raw physical honesty with hushed celestial abstraction.

### Aesthetic Movement
- **Archival / DIY Analog:** Xerox contact sheets, utilitarian catalog indexing, typewriter notations, razor-sharp paper cuts, and visible technical margins.
- **Subtle Cosmic (10% restraint):** Astronomical glyphs (`✦`, `*`, `⚬`), micro-scaled RA/DEC coordinates, faint elliptical traces, and spectral numbering without falling into retro-futurism or high-tech sci-fi.
- **Understated Intimacy:** Devoid of bright marketing callouts, promotional popups, or consumer urgency. The space feels like stumbling into an uncurated personal storage box or an underground venue's backroom desk.

### Tone of Voice & Copywriting
- Strictly eliminate commercial and e-commerce phrases (e.g., replace "Shop Now" with "Objects & Artifacts", "Add to Cart" with "Reserve Copy", "Sold Out" with "Depleted / In Private Hands").
- Write with quiet, observant cadence: lower-case fragments, field recording observations, equipment serials, date stamps, and meditative ephemera ("music keeps a place open.", "recorded on magnetic tape, late winter.").

## Colors

The chromatic language derives from soot-black darkrooms, fibrous unbleached paper, and the pale, distant luminescence of low-magnitude stars.

### Core Swatches
- **Base Canvas (Soot Black):** `#0c0d0e` (deep ground), `#121315` (primary surface), `#18191c` (secondary card / drawer surface), `#1e2024` (subtle elevated container or active state).
- **Text & Archival Ink (Warm Bone / Paper):** `#f3f2ee` (primary editorial body & titles), `#e4e2dc` (secondary body), `#9a9b9e` (muted catalog labels), `#6e7074` (metadata, deactivated items, faint footnotes).
- **Celestial Accents (Spectral Lavender-Blue):**
  - `#9da8c7` (Primary accent: active indicators, audio waveforms, subtle star glyphs).
  - `#b4bece` (Highlight: hover links, precise coordinate markers).
  - `#7e8cb3` (Deep accent: secondary glyphs, structural rules with tint).
- **Borders & Rules (Graphite & Etched Glass):**
  - Primary Border: `rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.12)` (crisp structure).
  - Secondary Hairline: `rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.06)` (internal cell dividers).
  - Accent Hairline: `rgba(157, 168, 199, 0.25)` (active audio focus or selected tape reel).

### Usage Rules
- Pure white (`#ffffff`) is strictly prohibited; all light reflections utilize `#f3f2ee` to simulate matte printing paper under low-voltage tungsten lamps.
- Accent colors must not exceed 5% of any viewport. They function as astronomical telemetry and delicate visual punctures rather than colored brand blocks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy operates across three distinct voices:
1. **The Poetic Record (`EB Garamond`):** Used for archival titles, song names, personal reflections, and editorial epigraphs. Set with classical rhythm, generous line heights, and delicate letter spacing. It evokes 18th-century paper presses, botanical classifications, and aged lyric booklets.
2. **The Humanist Voice (`Noto Sans`):** Clean, neutral, high-legibility Korean and multilateral reading text. Rendered in light weights (`300` and `400`) to remain unobtrusive and avoid clashing with the serif displays.
3. **The Index & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-pitch metadata, reel timestamps, field recording dates, geographical/astronomical coordinates, track durations, and catalog identification codes (`SKRN-004`). All mono text is rendered with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.18em`) and uppercase styling where appropriate.

### Typesetting Directives
- **Korean Typography:** In mixed Korean-English bodies, punctuation follows standard Korean typography while maintaining proportional spacing. Headings containing Korean glyphs alongside Roman characters use vertical metric optical centering.
- **Small Caps & Celestial Indicators:** Monospaced prefix numbers should always be paired with an archival indexer (e.g., `CATALOG NO. 03 ✦ [37°33'N 126°58'E]`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an asymmetrical 12-column architectural grid inspired by physical contact sheets, gallery exhibition binders, and xerox zines.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and a wide outer margin of `3rem`. Content is permitted to breathe inside uncentered offset columns (e.g., text occupying columns 2–6 while photo plates occupy columns 8–12, leaving column 7 as pure void).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margin. Layout collapses into vertical ledger sheets divided by hairline borders.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical negative space must feel intentional and unhurried. Generous intervals (`space-xl` and beyond) delineate movements between songs, physical artifact records, and journal fragments.
- Density is reserved for tabular index regions, tape tracklists, and technical specifications, contrasting vast empty voids with tight, precise information blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects drop shadows, blurred ambient lighting, and skeuomorphic glossy glass. Depth is produced strictly through tonal stratification and physical contact-sheet overlays.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (The Deep Floor / `#0c0d0e`):** The primary view background; evokes a pitch-black control room or starless night.
- **Tier 1 (The Mat Board / `#121315`):** Structural panels, editorial reading fields, and ledger columns.
- **Tier 2 (The Artifact Surface / `#18191c`):** Physical media card backings, audio visualizer enclosures, cassette sleeves.
- **Tier 3 (The Overlap / `#1e2024`):** Drawer menus, pull-out lyric sheets, photo loupe overlays.

### Hairline Borders over Shadows
- Elevation is defined exclusively by 1px rules:
  - Default containment: `1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.12)`.
  - Nested separators: `1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.06)`.
  - Focused item: `1px solid #9da8c7`.
- When an element floats (such as an audio player docked to the viewport bottom), it uses a crisp top border (`1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.12)`) and a dense backdrop blur of `12px` over `#0c0d0e` with 92% opacity.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly rectilinear (`0px` border radius throughout).

- **Raw Cuts:** Every button, image container, tab item, input, modal dialog, and thumbnail possesses true 90-degree corners, mirroring trimmed cardboard, slit film negatives, and razor-sliced paper.
- **Proportional Ratios:** Visual imagery conforms to tactile photographic standards: `3:2` (35mm negative), `1:1` (medium format vinyl/sleeve), or `4:5` (large format contact prints).
- **Notches & Tick Marks:** Corners may feature 1px coordinate ticks or crosshairs (`+`) at absolute vertices to reinforce archival cataloguing tools.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Form & Shape:** Strictly rectangular, zero corner radius.
- **Primary (Inverted Ledger Button):** `#f3f2ee` background with `#0c0d0e` typography in `label-code` (uppercase, tracking `0.12em`). On hover, background shifts to `#9da8c7` with smooth 150ms transition.
- **Secondary (Hairline Action):** Transparent ground, `1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.25)`, text in `#f3f2ee`. On hover, border shifts to `#f3f2ee` with an internal background tint of `rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost Utility Trigger:** No border, text in `#9a9b9e` prefixed with small star glyph (`✦ play audio`). On hover, text becomes `#9da8c7`.

### Lists & Tracklists
- **Structure:** Monospaced metadata columns separated by hairline horizontal rules (`rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.08)`).
- **Format:** `[Index: 01]` / `[Title: EB Garamond, 16px]` / `[Coordinates or Rec Location]` / `[Duration: 04:12]`.
- **Hover State:** Row background subtly illuminates to `#18191c` with a left border highlight of `2px solid #9da8c7`.

### Artifact Cards & Media Frames
- **Construction:** Background `#121315`, wrapped in `1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.12)`.
- **Image Treatment:** B&W or desaturated imagery with a slight analog warmth. A micro-label sits directly above or beneath the image containing serial code, date of capture, and negative type (`KODAK TX 400 - 1998.11.04`).
- **Content:** Headline in `EB Garamond`, followed by quiet description in `Noto Sans`, concluding with artifact status (`Archived / 1 cassette remaining`).

### Input Fields & Search / Ledger Registers
- **Styling:** Single bottom hairline border (`1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.2)`) with transparent background, or full flat box with `#0c0d0e` background and `1px` border.
- **Typography:** JetBrains Mono for user entries; placeholder text in `#6e7074` with lowercase phrasing (`e.g., enter postal address or note to band...`).
- **Focus:** Border transitions to `#9da8c7`; placeholder fades out completely.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Form:** Strictly square (14px x 14px) with `1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.3)`.
- **Active State:** Inside box fills with an etched cross (`✕`) or small solid square in `#9da8c7` rather than standard curved checks.

### Chips & Meta Tags
- **Structure:** Ultra-thin border (`1px solid rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.15)`), background transparent, zero border-radius.
- **Content:** JetBrains Mono, 10px, uppercase, padded with `2px 8px`. Prefix indicator: `⚬ LIVE TAPE`, `✦ 7" VINYL`, `REC: REHEARSAL RM 2`.

### Audio Scrub Bar & Cosmic Player
- **Design:** Persistent, razor-thin line docked to footer or within track drawer.
- **Timeline:** Background track `rgba(243, 242, 238, 0.1)`, active elapsed line `#9da8c7`.
- **Thumb:** 3px x 8px vertical slate bar (no round bubble), displaying current time and celestial declination in micro-monospace text.
