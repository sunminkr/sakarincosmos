From pickup request to collection

  1. Choose your items and pickup show

    Choose your items, quantities, and the upcoming show where you want to collect them. You can also submit items together from your cart.

  2. Enter your details and send the request

    Enter your name, mobile number, and the email address where you want to receive confirmation. Agree to the privacy terms and press the submit button. Your request will be emailed to the organizer.

  3. Receive and check your confirmation email

    The organizer will check item and pickup availability, then reply to the email address you entered. Your reservation is confirmed when you receive this email. Check the items, quantities, and pickup show. If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder too. Reply to the confirmation email if you need to make changes.

  4. Visit the merchandise booth on the day

    Visit the merchandise booth at your chosen show. Have your confirmation email ready so you can check your reservation details.

  5. Confirm your name and mobile number

    Tell the booth staff the name you used for the request and the last four digits of your mobile number. They will check your reservation and the items to be collected.

  6. Pay at the venue and collect your items

    Check the items, quantities, and amount due, then pay at the booth. Collect your items after payment and make sure they match your request.
